Executive Summary

Research Scope & Purpose

This comprehensive research covers 18+ CRM solutions specifically evaluated for commercial real estate professionals who need to manage complex workflows involving:

  • Properties & Portfolios: Multi-tenant buildings, multiple owners, diverse property types
  • Stakeholder Management: Owners, tenants, brokers, investors, and service providers
  • Deal Management: Lease negotiations, sales transactions, renewals, and expirations
  • Document Storage: Contracts, leases, financials, and marketing materials
  • Communication Tools: Email campaigns, SMS, dialer integration, and follow-up automation
  • Location Intelligence: Map views, territory management, and proximity analysis
