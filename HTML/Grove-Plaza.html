Key Investment Highlights & Value-Add Opportunity

  • Solid Anchor Tenant with Significant Upside: Acquire a property anchored by NAPA Auto Parts, a reputable national tenant, currently yielding a strong 7.08% cap rate. This provides stable in-place cash flow with immediate potential for substantial growth through lease-up.
  • Immediate Value-Add Potential: With the Bochetto Day Spa lease expiring in August 2025, an incoming investor gains direct control over 2,800 SF, in addition to the existing 4,200 SF vacancy, totaling 7,000 SF of prime leasable space. This substantial vacancy (52% of RBA) offers a clear path to significantly increasing returns.
  • Projected High Yield: Upon successful lease-up of the vacant 7,000 SF, the projected Net Operating Income (NOI) rises to $78,747, yielding an impressive 8.33% capitalization rate. This demonstrates a compelling value-add play, aligning with strategies for long-term portfolio growth.
  • Strategic Regional Connectivity: Located along Pacific Hwy with convenient access to Hwy 99 and I-5, this property benefits from strong local community engagement and regional accessibility. While Cottage Grove may be considered a secondary market, its excellent highway access and proximity to major throughfares enhance its appeal for national and regional tenants.
  • Recent Capital Improvements: Extensive renovations in 2024, including a new TPO roof with 12mm rigid insulation, two new HVAC units, and new exterior paint, significantly reduce immediate capital expenditure for a new owner, providing a modernized and turn-key opportunity for the existing NAPA tenancy.
  • Flexible Investment Strategy: This asset caters to both investors seeking long-term, stable NNN income from NAPA and those looking for a hands-on value-add play by aggressively repositioning the vacant spaces, reflecting a blend of in-place returns and future growth potential in line with diversified portfolios.
