From Lupe's Escape to Sherwin-Williams: A Transformation Story

This offering presents an excellent opportunity to acquire a well-located commercial property that has undergone a significant transformation. Originally home to Lupe's Escape, a popular local eatery, the property has now been repurposed as a Sherwin-Williams paint store, leased to one of the world's leading paint and coatings companies.

A prime investment with a strong tenant in a growing area, this property promises stable returns and long-term value.

Location Overview

The property is strategically situated along Tualatin Valley Hwy (Hwy 8), a major thoroughfare in Beaverton, Oregon, benefiting from excellent visibility and strong area demographics. With over 326,105 residents within a five-mile radius, the area is densely populated and continues to experience significant growth.

Nearby developments such as Reed's Crossing, Butternut Creek, and South Cooper Mountain further enhance the property's appeal by bringing in new residents and increasing commercial activity in the vicinity. The location offers easy access and is surrounded by a mix of retail, residential, and service-based businesses, making it a hub for local consumers.
